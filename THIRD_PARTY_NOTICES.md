## Device data

`src/fpga/devices/*.json` are generated by
`scripts/extract_device_profiles.py` from the device databases shipped in
the OSS CAD Suite: Project IceStorm (ISC), Project Trellis (ISC; database
CC0-1.0) and Apicula (MIT).

`plugins/fpga/hooks/scripts/safety_rail.py`, `protect_generated.py` and
the vision/profile hooks are adapted from `VibeBB/firmware-agent`
(BSD-3-Clause, same copyright holder).
